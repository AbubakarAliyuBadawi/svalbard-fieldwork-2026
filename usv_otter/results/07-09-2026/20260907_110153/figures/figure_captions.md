# Suggested captions and placement

All four figures are preliminary. Confirm the inferred decoder against a BioSonics export,
and confirm the site before attaching them to the report. Positions centre on
78.66364° N, 16.87848° E. No coastline or site name has been assumed.

1. Section 5.5.2, USV bathymetry results: Recorded Otter track coloured by stored
bottom range below the transducer. Grey points denote invalid bottom picks.
Coordinates use a local WGS84 azimuthal equidistant projection. The plot shows
measurements along the route, without interpolation between survey lines.

2. Section 5.5.2, after the track map: Bottom range versus cumulative observed
track distance (top) and elapsed recorder time (bottom). Invalid bottom picks
are omitted; range increases downward. Distance excludes intervals lacking
connected valid GPS fixes. No additional depth corrections have been applied.

3. Section 5.5.2 or appendix: Uncalibrated split-beam echo counts with stored
bottom picks overlaid in cyan. Colour represents log10 raw counts, not calibrated
backscatter. Every 5th ping is displayed for readability; the exported
NPZ retains every ping. Range is referenced to the transducer.

4. Section 5.5.3 or appendix: Successive ping intervals and stored bottom-pick
validity over the recording. These indicate recording continuity and tracker
output; they do not establish the timing or cause of a communication outage.
